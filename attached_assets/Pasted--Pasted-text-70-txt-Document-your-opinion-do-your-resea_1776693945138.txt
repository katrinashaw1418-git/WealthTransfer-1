Add more serious wealth/KYC fields before submission or immediately after the first step

That naming point matters because Google’s own guidance is for “Sign in with Google,” “Sign up with Google,” or “Continue with Google,” and it recommends using the official branded button rather than a homemade version. 

My recommendation is not to rely on Google sign-up as the only fast path. In regulated finance, Google authentication solves convenience, not identity. AUSTRAC’s guidance for individuals says you must collect full name, residential address, and date of birth, and verify full name plus either residential address or date of birth. AUSTRAC’s current onboarding examples also show collecting full name, residential address, date of birth, and additional information to establish ML/TF risk. 

That means your current page is missing some of the information that a proper wealth onboarding funnel usually asks for. Peer investment platforms also point the same way. Vanguard says it will typically ask for full name, date of birth, residential address, occupation, source of wealth, and citizenship, and its account-type pages for trusts and SMSFs point to collecting detailed trustee/director information. Betashares Direct explicitly asks for TFN or ABN because withholding can apply if it is not provided. Wealthfront and Wealthsimple also show that regulated investing apps usually require a permanent residential address, tax identifier, phone verification, and identity verification on top of account creation. 

So my recommendation for AMAX Wealth is:

Add Google sign-up: yes.
Is it mandatory? no.
Is it worth it? yes, for conversion and reduced friction. Google itself positions Sign in with Google as a way to simplify sign-up, reduce password friction, and improve conversion, and its current developer guidance says to place the button prominently and use the official SDK/button treatment. 

But the main upgrade you really need is the form itself.
