Critical corrections
1. FSG says AMAX Wealth holds AFSL — but your model says external retail AFSL partner

Your text says:

AMAX Wealth holds the AFSL
AMAX Wealth Pty Ltd is an Authorised Representative under an AFSL

Those are different legal positions. You must choose one.

If your plan is to cooperate with a retail AFSL wealth management company, safer wording is:

AMAX Wealth operates as a technology and platform provider. Financial product advice is provided by authorised representatives of the relevant AFSL holder. Adviser access is subject to approval, client permissioning, and the adviser’s licensing arrangements.

Do not say AMAX holds the AFSL unless the entity actually holds one.

2. “Wholesale investors only” conflicts with your retail-client adviser plan

You wrote:

personal advice to wholesale clients only
all products are available to wholesale investors only

But your current strategy is:

external financial planners provide advice for retail clients

That conflict must be fixed.

Use either:

Version A — wholesale-only platform

or:

Version B — retail clients through AFSL partner

For your current plan, use:

Retail clients may access investment products only where appropriate advice, disclosures, consent and platform approvals have been completed through the relevant AFSL holder.
3. Fee consent must include DBFO fields

Netwealth’s own fee consent guidance states the renewal period runs from 60 days before to 150 days after the reference day, and ongoing arrangements must include reference day and termination date; Netwealth’s DBFO note also states account numbers and services are required fields for ongoing fee consent.

So your feeConsents / fee UI should include:

account number
reference day
termination date
eligible services
consent signed date
expiry / termination date
renewal status
withdrawn at

Right now the UI says “Fee consents expiring ≤30d”, which is fine for dashboard alerts, but the actual fee page must track the full 60-before / 150-after DBFO window.

4. Investment instruction status shows “Consented” but says no funds move — good, but harden it

This is acceptable only if:

consented = terminal holding status for now

Do not allow:

consented → processing
processing → completed

until you build the execution worker and approval controls.

Add copy:

Client consent has been recorded. This instruction has not been executed. Processing remains disabled until AMAX platform approval and execution controls are enabled.
5. Product shelf is too crypto/digital-asset heavy

Your listed products:

Web3 Innovation Fund
Ethereum Staking Fund
Bitcoin Tracker Fund

This makes the adviser portal look like a crypto platform, not a Netwealth-style wealth platform.

For a retail AFSL planner channel, rebalance the product shelf:

Core Income Portfolio
Australian Property Income Fund
Corporate Credit Fund
Global Multi-Asset Portfolio
Cash Plus / Treasury Portfolio
Digital Assets Strategy — restricted / specialist only

Digital assets should be a specialist sleeve, not the lead product category.

6. Product return language is risky

You show:

Target Net IRR 25–35%
Target Net IRR 15.0%

That is dangerous on a product shelf unless supported by formal offer documents and risk disclosures.

Better labels:

Target return range
Illustrative target only
Not guaranteed
See PDS/IM

For retail-facing/adviser-facing display, add:

Targets are not forecasts or guarantees. Actual returns may be materially lower and capital loss is possible.
7. “AMAX does not hold client funds” conflicts with “AMAX holds cash custody”

The Wealth Planner document says AMAX holds cash custody; other copy says AMAX does not hold client funds.

You need one consistent model:

Client funds are held with regulated banking, custody, fund or product counterparties. AMAX records platform entitlements and instructions but does not hold client money as beneficial owner.
