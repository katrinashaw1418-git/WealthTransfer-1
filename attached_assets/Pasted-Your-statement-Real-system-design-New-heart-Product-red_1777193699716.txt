Thought for 16s

Yes — build AdviserOS end-to-end, but build it as a daily operating loop, not a set of disconnected pages.

Netwealth’s adviser platform is built around simplifying advice delivery, workflow, portfolio tools, digital consent, client reporting, practice management, research and customer tools. It also separates adviser/business functionality from personal investor access, which is the model AMAX should copy conceptually.
