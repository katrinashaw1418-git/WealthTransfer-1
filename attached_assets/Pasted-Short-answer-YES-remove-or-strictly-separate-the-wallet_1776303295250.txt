Short answer: **👉 YES — remove (or strictly separate) the “wallet” from your Wealth app.**

Given your structure (AMAX Global = AUSTRAC, AMAX Wealth = AFSL/AR), this is a **critical design decision**, not just UI.

---

# 🔑 1. Why “wallet” is dangerous in a Wealth app

In a **wealth management (AFSL) environment**, a “wallet” implies:

* Holding client money
* Stored value
* Payment functionality

👉 This creates **regulatory overlap** between:

* AUSTRAC (payments / DCE / remittance)
* ASIC (wealth / advice / investments)

---

## ⚠️ If you keep a wallet inside Wealth:

You risk:

### ❌ Client money handling issues

* Triggers **client money rules** under AFSL

### ❌ Regulatory confusion

* “Are you a payment provider or investment platform?”

### ❌ AFSL holder pushback

* Most AFSL holders will **NOT approve wallet features**

---

# 🏗️ 2. Correct structure (clean and scalable)

## 🔹 AMAX Global (keep wallet here)

👉 Wallet belongs ONLY here

* Fiat wallet
* Crypto wallet
* FX / transfers
* AUSTRAC regulated

---

## 🔹 AMAX Wealth (NO wallet)

👉 Wealth app should be:

* Portfolio
* Investments
* Advice
* Reporting

---

# 🧠 3. What to replace “wallet” with

Instead of “wallet”, use:

### ✅ “Investment Account” or “Portfolio”

This shows:

* Units held
* Value
* Performance

---

### NOT:

* ❌ Cash balance (like a bank account)
* ❌ Stored funds

---

# 🔁 4. How money should flow (correct model)

```id="flow1"
Client Bank Account
        ↓
External Custodian / Fund
        ↓
AMAX Wealth (record only)
        ↓
Portfolio display
```

---

## If using AMAX Global:

```id="flow2"
Client → AMAX Global (payment rails)
        ↓
External fund / custodian
        ↓
AMAX Wealth (reporting only)
```

---

👉 Wealth platform = **view + advice + instruction**
NOT = money holder

---

# ⚠️ 5. Exception (only if structured properly)

You can show a “cash allocation” IF:

* It is held with:

  * Custodian
  * Fund
  * Broker

👉 And clearly labelled:

* “Held with external provider”
* “Not held by AMAX”

---

# 💡 6. UI wording (VERY IMPORTANT)

## SAFE:

* “Portfolio value”
* “Investment holdings”
* “Cash allocation (held with custodian)”

---

## DANGEROUS:

* ❌ “Wallet balance”
* ❌ “Available funds”
* ❌ “Deposit / withdraw” inside wealth

---
